# Roadmap

Ideas for what's next, roughly in order of value.

1. **After-corner note in the game.** "Pouhon: braked 9 m early, -0.12 s" for a few seconds after each corner, in
   the CSP app (brake boards, racing line, 3D ghost and the gap shipped in 2.2).
2. **Consistency ghost and ghost files.** A ghost of your typical lap, and a file to swap laps with a friend.
3. **Real pedal data for ghosts.** Check whether CSP exposes other cars' throttle and brake online; if so, send them
   and stop estimating, which makes brake boards from other drivers' laps exact (today: within 4 m nine times out of ten).
4. **Get it in front of people.** Post on Overtake.gg (formerly RaceDepartment) with screenshots and the download.
5. **Shared ghost library (later).** Opt-in upload of fast laps so ghosts exist without racing those drivers.
   Needs hosting and privacy decisions; only worth it once people use Ghostline.
