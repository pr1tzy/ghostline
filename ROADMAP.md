# Roadmap

Where Ghostline goes next, in order. What already shipped is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

1. **Corner notes in the game.** A line after every corner while you drive: "Pouhon: braked 9 m early, -0.12 s",
   on the dash for a few seconds.
2. **Ghost files and a consistency ghost.** Send a lap to a friend and race their ghost; a ghost of your typical
   lap, not just your best one.
3. **Real pedals for every ghost.** Other drivers' throttle and brake straight from the game where Custom Shaders
   Patch exposes them online, so brake boards from their laps are exact instead of estimated (today: within 4 m
   nine times out of ten).
4. **A signed installer.** No "Windows protected your PC" on the first run. The release workflow is ready for
   signing; it needs a certificate.
5. **A shared ghost library.** Opt-in: fast laps for every car and track without having raced those drivers first.
   Needs hosting and a privacy design that keeps Ghostline's "nothing leaves your PC" default.
