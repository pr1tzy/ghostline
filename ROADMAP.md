# Roadmap

Ideas for after the Windows installer, roughly in order of value.

1. **In-game HUD (CSP app).** Delta to the ghost, "Ascari -0.12 vs ghost" after each corner and the predicted lap,
   inside the game, so a second monitor isn't needed. The CSP app already runs in the game.
2. **Ghost on the track.** Draw the ghost's position in the 3D world through CSP, like a racing game's ghost car.
3. **Real pedal data for ghosts.** Check whether CSP exposes other cars' throttle and brake online; if so, send them
   and stop estimating, which makes braking-point advice exact.
4. **Get it in front of people.** Post on Overtake.gg (formerly RaceDepartment) with screenshots and the download.
5. **Shared ghost library (later).** Opt-in upload of fast laps so ghosts exist without racing those drivers.
   Needs hosting and privacy decisions; only worth it once people use Ghostline.
